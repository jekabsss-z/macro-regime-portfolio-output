Algorithm lab 🧪

Write a score formula. Each month the tool ranks every ETF in a sleeve by your score (point-in-time trailing history — no look-ahead), picks the top N (from the Auto-rules table below), weights them, and rebalances. Higher score = preferred. Variables per ETF: mom3 mom6 mom12 (trailing returns), vol (ann. volatility), dd (trailing max drawdown, negative), sharpe, ret1 (last month).
